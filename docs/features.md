# What you can do with FaizX

[← Overview](../README.md) · [Connect your TV](connecting.md)

## Control without hunting for buttons

The circular remote keeps directional movement and selection together. Back and Home, TV settings, app shortcuts and the system Cast shortcut provide quick routes to common actions. The typing field sends text to the focused TV input; voice entry uses the phone's speech-recognition service.

Pointer mode uses the same control area. Its availability depends on the TV's supported input mechanism and an authorised ADB connection. Separate directional rails give you explicit up/down and left/right navigation instead of relying on pointer position alone.

Power actions are requests to the connected TV. Screen wake, standby, restart and shutdown support varies by firmware and connection. Confirm the selected TV before using them.

## A workspace for the whole device

- **App library:** browse installed apps, find an app and open supported management actions.
- **Device health:** review CPU, RAM and storage readings reported by the TV. Per-app data can be restricted by the system; unavailable readings should not be interpreted as zero usage.
- **Storage:** move between apps and files, inspect internal storage and supported connected drives, and transfer content between phone and TV.
- **Device care:** review available maintenance and app-management actions. Disabling a system component can affect TV functions; the TV and the app's safeguards determine what is available.

## Files that move with you

Browse the relevant TV storage, select a file or folder and use the available file actions. The destination must be writable. To send a file, select it through the phone's picker and choose the destination offered by the app. For a file already on TV, use the available download or preview action.

File transfer and media playback are different capabilities. A completed transfer does not guarantee the TV can decode that video's codec, audio format or resolution. The player, TV firmware and connected storage all affect playback.

## Capture and share

Where supported, screenshot and recording tools let you choose a destination. Capture depends on the active connection and the TV's encoder and display restrictions. Streaming services can protect their content from capture.

## Connect with FaizX TV OS

The Companion is the phone app. FaizX TV OS is the launcher on the television. Launcher installation, wallpapers and private update options require the corresponding compatible launcher or release service. They are not necessary for basic native Wi-Fi remote control.

## Designed with permission boundaries

Native Wi-Fi pairing and ADB authorisation are distinct. Update access can require separate owner approval. Grant only the permissions needed for the feature you are using, and keep TV debugging limited to trusted devices and networks.
