About the app
FaizX TV Companion lets you remotely control and manage compatible televisions. Features vary by TV, firmware, permissions and connection method.

Authorised use
Connect only to devices you own or have permission to manage. Keep pairing codes, PINs and access credentials private. Review the selected TV before sending commands or files.

Changes you request
App removal, file deletion, factory resets and other administrative actions can remove data or change your TV. Review prompts and back up important files before proceeding. Only record or share content you have permission to capture and distribute.

Updates and third-party services
App and launcher releases may require a paired connection to an authorised update service. Android handles installation approvals. Casting, speech recognition and external links use their respective providers and are subject to their terms and privacy practices.
