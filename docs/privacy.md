# Privacy Policy

[← Overview](../README.md)

Who this covers
This notice applies to FaizX TV Companion for Android. It describes the phone app and its release-service connection. Other apps or services on your phone or TV have their own policies.

Local TV connections
Companion uses your local network to discover and communicate with the TV you select. It can read the TV address, device status, installed app list, storage and other device information needed for the controls you use. ADB and supported Wi-Fi remote connections require authorisation or pairing. Pairing state, preferences and history may be stored on your phone.

Text, files and recordings
Text and remote input are sent to the selected TV. When you choose a transfer, screenshot or recording, Companion reads or creates the requested item and sends or saves it at the destination you choose. Files you save can remain on the phone, TV or attached storage after you uninstall Companion.

Voice and Cast
Voice input opens your phone’s speech recognition service when requested. The recognition provider may process audio on your device or send it for processing under its own policy. Recognised text appears in the editor before you send it. Cast opens Android’s system controls and uses the services supported by your phone.

Release services and external links
Update checks, pairing and authorised downloads can contact the FaizX release service over HTTPS. The service processes network request metadata, release information and paired-device authorisation records to deliver updates and protect access. External pages and configured data sources receive requests when used. Their own privacy policies apply.

Retention and deletion
You can remove saved connections, clear Companion storage or uninstall the app to remove app-local data. Revoke the phone’s remote or debugging authorisation on the TV separately. Remove files saved outside Companion from their destination. Release-service authorisation records may remain until revoked or removed by the service operator. For questions or requests about those records, use the privacy or support contact supplied with your download.

Diagnostics
Settings → Support lets you preview a diagnostic report. Review it before sending it to anyone.
